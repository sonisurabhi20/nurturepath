# Printable Activity Requirements

Section IDs are stable citation labels for the prototype.

## PT-1 · Required fields

Every printable specification includes: title, age band, purpose, materials, estimated time, adult setup, numbered steps, supervision, safety notes, and source activity ID.

## PT-2 · Approved page types

The prototype may specify a weekly overview, individual activity card, shared sibling card, coloring page, match-and-circle page, routine-sequencing page, large-piece sticking page, simple tracing page for the older child, or parent-cut picture cards. Every page must map to an approved catalog activity.

## PT-3 · Younger-child requirements

For ages 18–24 months, use large high-contrast elements, one simple action at a time, and constant adult supervision. No small loose pieces are allowed. The adult completes all cutting before the child participates.

## PT-4 · Older-child requirements

For ages 36–48 months, simple coloring, matching, sequencing, large-piece pasting, and basic tracing may be used when allowed by the activity catalog. The adult still completes all cutting.

## PT-5 · Print behavior

Pages default to black-and-white and ink-friendly, with color as an optional presentation choice. The caregiver may select a complete packet or specific pages only after the applicable approval or acknowledgment gate.
