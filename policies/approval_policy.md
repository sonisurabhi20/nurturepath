# Parent Approval and Memory Policy

Section IDs are stable citation labels for the prototype.

## AP-1 · Inactive until review

Every at-home plan is a draft after the agent's quality check. It cannot become active or printable for use until the caregiver clicks **Approve**. Available review actions are Approve, Edit, Reject, or Escalate.

## AP-2 · Edited plans are rechecked

A caregiver edit returns the entire draft through age-fit, catalog, safety, completeness, route, prohibited-language, and approval checks. Edited text is never activated automatically.

## AP-3 · Professional-review acknowledgment

A non-urgent professional-review warning must be acknowledged before any general low-risk activity can unlock. Acknowledgment does not approve an activity and does not replace professional discussion.

## AP-4 · Urgent and policy-review locks

An urgent-stop cannot be approved, edited into an at-home plan, printed, or bypassed. A policy-review case stays paused until the simulated reviewer resolves it.

## AP-5 · No autonomous external action

The agent cannot contact a professional, emergency service, school, or third party; send data; change an external record; or take any consequential action. The caregiver remains responsible for real-world decisions.

## AP-6 · Bounded memory

Remember only parent-approved plans, tried activities, parent-reported outcomes, prior check-in summaries, warning acknowledgments, and escalation status from the selected synthetic household. Do not remember unapproved drafts, unsaved conversation, inferred conditions, or another household's information.
